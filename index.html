The claim, precisely

One axiom, one polyhedron, integer inputs

Treat space as an infinite pre-existing foam at the Planck scale. Every event is a displacement in that foam: B + V = D. Fill space with equal cells at minimum surface area and the cell is forced — it is the truncated octahedron (Kelvin cell). Its face Laplacian has a spectrum fixed entirely by the cell's integers: \(|O_h| = 48\), \(V = 24\), \(E = 36\), \(F = 14\) (8 hexagons, 6 squares), \(d = 3\). The Standard Model's dimensionless ratios are read off that spectrum.

Falsifiability

How to kill it

The framework is rigid: changing any cell integer by ±1 moves a headline number by ≥1%. That rigidity is the point — here is what a single measurement would have to do.

01

Find a dark-matter particle

UFFT says dark matter is lattice anisotropy, not a particle — LZ, XENON and ADMX stay empty. One confirmed WIMP or axion and the picture is wrong.

02

Find supersymmetry

Fourteen faces give exactly the Standard Model gauge content, with no room for superpartners. A single confirmed sparticle, at any energy, falsifies it.

03

Pin α to rubidium

\(\alpha^{-1} = 137.035999055\): 0.3σ from the caesium value, 1.4σ from rubidium. Confirm the rubidium value past 3σ and the closed form is dead.

04

Break the colour ratio

\(\delta_{PMNS}/\delta_{CKM} = 3\) exactly, from the colour factor \(C_A=3\). DUNE measures \(\delta_{PMNS}\). A confirmed ratio ≠ 3 at >3σ ends it.
